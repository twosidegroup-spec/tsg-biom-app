How the real scanning works

TSG Biom is palm-only. MediaPipe landmarks locate the palm, a square palm image is cut out and aligned, and an EDCC palmprint encoder reads the palm's own print pattern — the method family used by real palmprint systems. Enrolment banks the individual print codes from the best fully-open frames. At verification the live palm image is re-encoded at 25 small shifts and the best match against the enrolled codes decides — the score-level max rule. Nothing here is simulated — if no hand or no clear print is seen, the scan fails openly. Palm-vein scanning needs infrared hardware, which a normal camera does not have — we do not fake it.

Step 2 — Palm scan (real palmprint)

Tap Auto-Capture Palm and follow the movement prompts as they appear — the sequence is picked at random each time, and a photo cannot perform it, so a single photo is never accepted. Hold the phone about 30 cm away so the whole hand fits inside the ring; a palm pressed close to the lens cannot be read. The app watches live: the movement is checked, the palm square is cut out and aligned from the hand landmarks, and an EDCC palmprint encoder reads the palm's own print pattern from the best fully-open frames — the individual print codes are stored. Labelled honestly: this is Palm — Palmprint, the print-pattern method real palmprint systems use. Palm-vein scanning needs infrared hardware, which a normal camera does not have — we do not fake it.
