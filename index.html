How the real scanning works

Face: a real face-recognition model extracts a 128-point descriptor; verification measures the actual distance between scans. Palm: MediaPipe reads 21 hand landmarks and measures hand geometry. Fingerprint: a camera photographs your fingertip and the system enhances and matches the real ridge pattern from those pixels. Nothing here is simulated — if no face, hand or clear print is seen, the scan fails openly. Enroll any one, two, or all three - each biometric works independently.

Step 4 — Fingerprint through the camera (real)

Defaults to the back camera — you can switch to the front camera with the toggle below. Hold your fingertip facing the camera, 5–8 cm away, filling the guide, in good light, and keep still. Partner Integration

Biom Pay authorizes a payment biometrically on this terminal. Real money moves only through the official bKash / Nagad merchant APIs, after TwoSide Group signs the collaboration and the store adds its merchant credentials here. Until then, transactions are recorded truthfully as biometric-authorized, pending partner settlement — TSG Biom never invents a bKash / Nagad transaction ID.
